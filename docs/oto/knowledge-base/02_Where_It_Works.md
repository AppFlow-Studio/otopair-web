# Where Otopair Works

## Staten Island: live first

- **Staten Island, New York City** is Otopair's first and, today, only live borough
- The whole island is covered, from St. George to Tottenville. Each shop's page names its neighborhood, and a neighborhood without its own shop books the nearest one on the map
- Every shop is reviewed and approved by Otopair's team, and has to finish its setup, before drivers can book it
- Drivers don't have to live on Staten Island: drivers from anywhere can download the app and book a Staten Island shop
- Why Staten Island first: it has the highest car ownership of any New York City borough, and it's where the Otopair team is based
- The live list of verified shops is on the website at otopair.com/shops (see "Shops on Otopair")

## Coverage timeline

- **Staten Island** — live now
- **Brooklyn** — planned for Q4 2026
- **Queens** — planned for Q1 2027
- **The Bronx** — planned for Q2 2027
- **Manhattan** — planned for Q3 2027

Otopair opens New York City one borough at a time. A borough opens on readiness, not on a marketing date: only once enough verified shops are on the network there to book from. That's why these are plans, not promises. Repair shops anywhere in NYC can apply now, and shops verified ahead of their borough's quarter are bookable the day it opens.

## Sign-up and borough waitlists

- **Driver onboarding** — a visitor can leave their email with Oto or on the site to get an onboarding link, or download the app directly on iOS or Android. If they decoded a VIN with Oto, the car is attached when they sign up with the same email
- **Borough waitlists** — the Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx and Manhattan pages on the site each have their own waitlist that emails you when that borough's first shops go live

## Outside NYC

Otopair operates in New York City only, and nothing beyond the five boroughs has been announced. Drivers from anywhere can book a shop in a live borough — today that's Staten Island.

## How to answer "is Otopair in my area?"

- **In Staten Island:** "Yes — Staten Island is live right now, and the whole island is covered. You can download the Otopair app or sign up here to book with verified shops."
- **Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx or Manhattan:** "Not yet — [borough] is planned for [quarter]. A borough opens once enough verified shops are on the network there. The [borough] page on our site has a waitlist that emails you when the first shops go live, and you can also book a Staten Island shop right now in the app."
- **Outside NYC:** "Right now Otopair is New York City only, and nothing beyond the five boroughs has been announced. If you can get your car to Staten Island, you can book a shop there today in the app."

**"When is Otopair coming to Queens?"**
"Queens is planned for Q1 2027. That's the current plan rather than a promise — a borough opens once it has enough verified shops. The Queens page has a waitlist that emails you when the first shops go live."

**"Where can I download the app?"**
"The Otopair app is live on both the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android. You can sign up right here to get started, or download it from your app store."

## Important boundaries

- Give only the planned quarter from the timeline above, and always say "planned" — never a month or a day, and never a guarantee
- Do **not** say other cities or states are planned or on the roadmap
- Verified shops on Staten Island include Eltingville Auto Care, Precision Motors, and Forest Ave German; for the full roster, point to otopair.com/shops ([Staten Island Shops](/shops))
- Direct users to download the app or sign up to book their repair with locked pricing
