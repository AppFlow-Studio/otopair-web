## Refuted candidates (pipeline right or defensible — do NOT ship fixes)

1. **ATF part 00232-19057 "ATF SP-IV M" — NOT a trap-3 hit.** Verified: 00232-19057 is genuine Hyundai **ATF SP-IV M (SP4M)** (hyundai.oempartsonline, hyundaipartsdeal, Amazon). GT trap 3 says "only SP-IV is right," but a Hyundai TSB (NHTSA SB-10076424) designates SP4-M as the service fluid for the 6-speed automatics, and SP-IV/SP4M are treated as interchangeable for the A6MF2; the stored fluid SPEC string is exactly "Hyundai ATF SP-IV". The wrong answer would have been SP4M-1 (NX4 8AT) — not stored. Defensible; no defect.
2. **Orchestrator hypothesis "28113-D3300 is the NX4 part" — REFUTED.** 28113-D3300 is the **TL** engine air filter: fits 2016-2021 Tucson incl. 2.4L 2018-2021 (Amazon OEM listings, hyundai.oempartsonline "2016-2021 Hyundai Tucson Air Filter", hyundaioempartsdirect, crosses CA12065/WA10335 per GT row 6a). The refute of it was therefore a FALSE POSITIVE — see P1-2. The refute is NOT the round-9 year-band rule "working"; it is the rule firing on a wrong chassis premise.
