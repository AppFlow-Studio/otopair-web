# Pricing

## Overview

Pricing on Otopair is transparent. Drivers see the full price for their car **before they book** — labor, parts, and taxes broken out, and one locked total. No hidden fees, nothing added at checkout, no upsells without explicit approval.

## What shows on the receipt

At checkout and on the final receipt, the driver sees:

- **Labor** — labor for the specific service on the specific vehicle
- **Parts** — OEM or OEM-equivalent parts
- **Taxes** — calculated based on New York rules
- **Your locked price** — the total. Otopair's fees are already included in it; nothing is added on top at checkout

## Parts pricing — a positive trust moment

When a quote is given, parts are quoted at the **high end** of the expected range. Real-world parts costs can land anywhere in a range, and Otopair never wants a driver to see a number go up after the fact. When the actual parts cost comes in lower than quoted, **the driver pays less**. That is a positive trust moment by design.

## OEM parts only

For the standard service catalog, Otopair uses **OEM or OEM-equivalent parts**. Parts used are logged after every job and visible in service history.

## Pricing locks at confirmation

Once the driver confirms a booking, the price is **locked**. If the mechanic discovers something additional during the visit (for example, during a diagnostic), the shop has to send the driver a **new quote** and the driver has to **approve it** before any new work or charge happens. No surprise add-ons.

## Walk-in pricing

If a driver walks into an Otopair partner shop without an appointment, the shop offers two paths:

- **Pay on App** — Get the Otopair app, get a discount on this visit, and start tracking service history right away
- **Pay at Shop** — Pay the shop directly, and a text link follows so the visit can attach to a future service history

## Taxes

NY sales tax is calculated at checkout and shown as its own line. State inspection fee (where applicable) is also shown as a separate line.

## How to answer common questions

**"How much does X cost?"**
"Depends on the service and your car. In the Otopair app you see the full locked price for your car before you book — labor, parts, and taxes included, and nothing gets added later. The app is launching soon; join the launch list to be first to try it."

**"Does Otopair charge a fee?"**
"Our fees are already inside the price you see. Nothing gets tacked on at checkout — the locked price is what you pay."

**"Why are parts quoted high?"**
"Because we'd rather under-promise and over-deliver. If the real parts cost comes in lower than the quote, you pay less. We never want a price to go up after the fact."

## Important boundaries — DO NOT DISCLOSE

The following are **internal** and the voice agent must NOT explain them:

- **Do not** state, estimate, or hint at Otopair's fee amount or percentage — fees are included in the locked price
- **Do not** describe how labor cost is calculated (the internal formula for combining shop rate and labor time)
- **Do not** explain the difference between how tire pricing and non-tire pricing are determined internally
- **Do not** quote specific dollar amounts for services
- **Do not** describe internal cost structures, vendor relationships, or platform economics
- **Do not** reveal walk-in discount percentages beyond what the shop posts in person

The customer-facing answer to "how is pricing decided" is always: "The shop sets the price for the job, and you see one locked total for your car before you book — nothing gets added later."
