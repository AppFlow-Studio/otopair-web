## Adversarial traps

1. **#1 TRAP — trans-fluid family:** the Sport badge existed as BOTH 1.5T/CVT and 2.0T/10AT in 2022. A 1.5T answering **ATF Type 2.0** (or generic "Honda ATF") = 2.0T bleed-through; **ATF Type 3.1** = wrong Honda transmission entirely (ZF 9AT); **DW-1** = pre-2018 conventional AT; **HMMF** = old Honda CVTs. Only **HCF-2** passes for the 1.5T.
2. **Oil-capacity swap:** 5.0-5.1 qt on a 1.5T VIN (or 3.7 qt on a 2.0T) is the single most likely same-platform wrong-engine number. Band for the 1.5T: 3.5-3.7 qt only.
3. **Civic L15B7 contamination:** L15B7 as the engine code, or plug 12290-59B-003 (ILZKAR8H8S), is the Civic/CR-V 1.5T variant — plausible-looking but wrong for the Accord's L15BE (correct plug 12290-6A0-A01 / DILKAR8P8SY).
4. **Plug-price sanity check:** genuine 10th-gen plugs are ~$31-46 each. A $6-10 plug price band usually means an older/Civic part was priced instead.
5. **Reverse trap — shared parts:** oil filter (15400-PLM-A02) and spark plug (12290-6A0-A01) are genuinely SHARED between 1.5T and 2.0T. Do not flag these as cross-engine contamination; the discriminators are oil capacity, trans fluid, air filter (6A0 vs 6B2), and coolant capacity.
6. **11th-gen (2023+) parts:** 2023 moved to the 11th gen (1.5T/CVT continues; 2.0T DROPPED in favor of the hybrid). Air/cabin filters and body parts differ; the plug 12290-6A0-A01 happens to carry over (fitment through 2025). Check each part's fitment years rather than assuming all-2023 parts fit or all fail.
7. **Coolant-capacity inversion:** on this platform the 1.5T holds MORE coolant (6.3 L) than the 2.0T (5.06 L). An enricher "correcting" 6.3 L downward because the engine is smaller is inventing an error. Also: Type 2 blue only.
8. **Oil-dilution recall noise:** the 1.5T fuel-dilution recall/warranty-extension campaigns (TSB 18-114/18-115 etc.) covered **2016-2018 Civic and 2017-2018 CR-V** — NOT the 2022 Accord. Owner complaints exist for 2018 Accords, but a "recall" or shortened factory oil interval claimed for a 2022 Accord 1.5T is fabricated; intervals stay Maintenance-Minder-driven.
9. **Timing belt line = FAIL:** L15BE is chain/belt-in-oil design with no scheduled belt replacement; MM code 4 on this engine = plugs + valve-clearance inspection only.
10. **PS fluid line = FAIL:** dual-pinion EPS, no hydraulic fluid. Brake fluid is time-based (3 years), not mileage-based — a pure-mileage brake-fluid interval is a semantics miss.
11. **CVT capacity semantics:** 3.9 qt (up to ~4.5 qt to level) is the drain-and-refill figure; **8.8 qt is total/dry capacity** — a service line quoting ~9 qt is a capacity-semantics error, not a bigger transmission.
12. **Battery decoy:** Group 51R = 9th-gen Accord carryover; 10th gen is Group 47 (H5).
