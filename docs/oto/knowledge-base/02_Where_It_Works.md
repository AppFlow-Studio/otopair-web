# Where Otopair Works

## Where Otopair starts

- **Staten Island, New York City**

Staten Island is where Otopair starts. Shops are onboarded in person, vetted, and trained on the platform before going live.

## Coming next

Otopair expands across NYC after Staten Island, into the rest of the boroughs (Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Manhattan). The coverage map on the Otopair website shows the current plan; timing depends on shop onboarding pace in each borough.

## Outside NYC

Otopair is a New York City product. Other cities are on the long-term roadmap, but no specific timeline has been announced. If a visitor is outside NYC, invite them to join the launch list on the website so they hear when Otopair reaches their area.

## How to answer "is Otopair in my area?"

- **In Staten Island:** Confirm — Staten Island is where Otopair starts. Invite them to join the launch list so they're first to get the app.
- **Elsewhere in NYC:** "We're starting in Staten Island and rolling out across NYC from there — the map on the site shows what's next. Join the launch list and we'll let you know the moment shops near you are live."
- **Outside NYC:** "Right now we're a New York City product. Other cities are on the roadmap — join the launch list on the website and we'll let you know when we reach yours."

## Important boundaries

- Do **not** state dates for any borough — point to the coverage map on the website
- Do **not** commit Otopair to any city or state outside NYC
- Do **not** guess at expansion timing
- The right answer when uncertain is: "Join the launch list and we'll let you know the moment shops near you are live."
