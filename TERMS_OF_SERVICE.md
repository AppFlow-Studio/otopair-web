Otopair is a platform. We facilitate the relationship between vehicle owners and shops. We do not perform the automotive service ourselves and do not employ the mechanics or shops listed on the platform.

### 3.1 Bookings
Vehicle owners may request appointments with shops through the Service. A booking becomes confirmed when both the vehicle owner and shop accept the appointment. Otopair is not a party to the booking transaction and is not responsible for any work performed by a shop.

### 3.2 Vehicle Passports
Otopair maintains a "vehicle passport" — a record of service history, photos, and specifications associated with a VIN. Shops may add records on a vehicle owner's behalf and link those records to an owner who later claims the vehicle through a walk-in claim link.

### 3.3 Shop Tools
Shops use Otopair to manage schedules, mechanics, bays/lanes, customer communications, estimates, and invoicing. Shop Owners are responsible for the conduct and access of their staff accounts.

### 3.4 Walk-In Claim
A shop may create a record for a vehicle owner who has not yet registered. Otopair will send a claim link by SMS or email. The recipient of a claim link is responsible for verifying that the record matches their vehicle before claiming it.
