## Adversarial traps (wrong-answer decoys the pipeline may ingest)

1. **Base 2.0L Nu contamination (same model year, same manual)**: oil 4.23 qt (4.0 L); ATF 7.71 qt 2WD / 7.50 qt AWD (A6GF1); coolant 7.93 qt. These sit in the SAME OM tables one column over from the 2.4 values — an extractor that drops the engine qualifier emits them for the Ultimate. Oil filter and plug part numbers are shared across both engines, so part-number match alone cannot confirm correct-engine sourcing. Also note engine-code noise: the OM fuse chart says the 2.0 is **G4NC**, while most third-party sources say G4NA — either way, any "G4N*" code on the Ultimate 2.4 = wrong engine.
2. **4th-gen NX4 (2022+) bleed — the highest-volume trap**: 2.5L Smartstream (G4KN) + 8AT, **0W-20 oil (5.8 L)**, **ATF SP4M-1**, cartridge oil filter 26350-2S001, **DOT-4 LV Class-6** brake fluid, hybrid/PHEV 1.6T variants. The NX4 launched April 2021, so "2021-dated" content is often about the 2022 model; KBB already serves 0W-20 for "2021 Tucson". Even the manual-mirror site htmanual.net is the NX4 manual despite the generic "Hyundai Tucson" title. Hybrid/inverter-coolant/e-CVT/8AT content anywhere = wrong generation — **no hybrid Tucson existed for MY2021 US**.
3. **SP-III vs SP-IV vs SP4M-1**: SP-III is the older Hyundai 4/5-speed ATF — abundant in Hyundai search results and wrong for the A6MF2 (correct = SP-IV, verbatim OM). SP4M/SP4M-1 is the later 8AT-era fluid (NX4) — "close name, wrong car." A fluid answer is only right at exactly SP-IV.
4. **Theta II recall noise**: G4KJ/Theta II is saturated with 2011-2019 engine-seizure recall/class-action content (Sonata/Santa Fe short-block replacements, knock-sensor KSDS campaigns). Recall pages mention oil specs, oil-consumption tests, and part numbers for OTHER Theta II applications — none of it is maintenance ground truth for this Tucson, and "5W-30 only after recall" forum claims are not the OM spec.
5. **Early-TL (2016-2018) same-generation bleed**: 1.6 T-GDI (G4FJ) + **7-speed EcoShift DCT** — 45,000-mi spark plugs, GL-4 70W dual-clutch fluid, H5/Group 47 or 124R batteries. Fitment pages that say "2016-2021 Tucson" without engine splits pull these in; the 2021 US lineup was 2.0/2.4 + 6AT only.
6. **Euro/other-market TL specs**: garage.wiki's "2021 Tucson third-generation facelift" page has NO 2.4 at all (1.6 GDI/T-GDI, 1.7/2.0 CRDi diesels, 48V MHEV) — wrong-market data that looks generation-correct. US-market OM is the only authority for the 2.4.
7. **Look-wrong-but-correct values (do NOT "fix")**: cabin filter replaced every **7,500 mi/12 mo** (verbatim, aggressive); engine air filter replace at **22,500-mi** multiples (not the folk "30k"); ATF **"No check, No service required"** under normal usage (verbatim) yet **replace at 60k under severe**; brake fluid **replaced every 30k/48 mo** (Hyundai does schedule it, unlike Toyota); coolant first change deferred to **120k mi/10 yr** but then a much shorter 30k/24-mo cadence; severe-schedule oil at the odd-looking **3,750 mi/6 mo**.
