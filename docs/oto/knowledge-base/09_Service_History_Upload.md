# Service History Upload

## Overview

Drivers can upload PDFs of past service records from any prior shop or dealer. The more history Otopair knows about a vehicle, the more accurate its maintenance recommendations — and the better Oto can scope a job when something comes up.

## How it works

- Open the vehicle profile and tap to upload records
- PDFs from any prior shop, dealer, or service center are accepted
- The app shows an expandable preview of each upload so the driver can confirm what they sent
- Uploaded records sit on the car's profile in the driver's account

## Or just tell Oto

No receipt? In the app, a driver can tell Oto about mileage, warning lights or a service done elsewhere. Oto shows a confirm card, and nothing is written to the car's record until the driver confirms. Once it's on the record, the Maintenance Tracker and the Vehicle Health Score move with it, and the next shop sees it too.

## Other ways records reach the car

- **Otopair bookings** — every job, receipt and inspection done through Otopair stays with the vehicle
- **A walk-in visit** — if an Otopair shop already serviced the car as a walk-in, the shop may have sent a claim link by text or email; claiming it attaches those records to the driver's account
- **The per-VIN record** — separately, Otopair keeps a per-VIN record of what shops have physically confirmed about the car, such as mileage, tires, fluids, brakes and inspection status, with the date of the last shop confirmation. That record travels with the car, not with the owner

## What Otopair does with the records

Service history feeds the maintenance intelligence engine:

- Avoids recommending services that were recently completed elsewhere
- Helps the engine understand prior wear and confidence
- Sharpens recommendations for things like brake fluid, transmission service, and coolant flush, which are time-based and easy to forget
- Helps Oto scope the job when a symptom comes up later

## Privacy

- Records sit on the vehicle's profile in the driver's account and feed that car's maintenance recommendations and shop intake
- Otopair does not sell personal information
- Repair and service data stripped of anything that identifies the driver, the car or the shop may be combined with other records to build Otopair's aggregate vehicle-data products, such as typical service intervals for a model
- Drivers can delete records from the vehicle profile at any time, and can ask for their data to be deleted (see "Privacy, Security and the Terms")

## The more you share, the more accurate it gets

Otopair never penalizes a driver for not uploading records — but the recommendations get noticeably more accurate when prior history is on file. A driver with no history gets conservative recommendations based on age and mileage assumptions. A driver with full history gets precise, non-duplicative recommendations.

## How to answer common questions

**"Should I upload my service records?"**
"Yes — uploading past records helps us give better recommendations because we understand what's already been done on your car. It's optional but encouraged."

**"What files can I upload?"**
"PDFs — receipts, work orders, or summaries from any prior shop or dealer. No receipt? In the app you can just tell Oto what was done, and confirm it."

**"What happens to my records?"**
"They live on your car's profile and power its maintenance recommendations. Otopair doesn't sell personal information — records with everything identifying stripped out can feed Otopair's aggregate car data, like typical service intervals for a model. You can delete records from the profile anytime, and the privacy page has the details."

**"If I sell my car, does its history go with it?"**
"The record of what Otopair shops have physically confirmed about the car — mileage, tires, fluids, brakes, inspection status — is kept per VIN, so that part travels with the car, not the owner."

## Important boundaries

- Do **not** promise that uploaded records change the price of services
- Do **not** say records are used only for recommendations
- Do **not** describe internal storage architecture
- Do **not** ask the user to upload sensitive personal documents — only service records
