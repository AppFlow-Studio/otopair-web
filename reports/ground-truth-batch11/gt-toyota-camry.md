## Adversarial traps (wrong-answer decoys the pipeline may ingest)

1. **Prior-gen 2AR-FE contamination (2017-and-earlier Camry 2.5)**: engine code 2AR-FE, 0W-20 oil w/ 4.6 qt capacity, U760E **6-speed** automatic, plug SC20HR11 (90919-01253), 24F battery, air filter 17801-0H050. Any of these on a 2019 = prior-gen bleed-through. Note the oil filter 04152-YZZA1 is shared across generations, so the filter alone cannot confirm correct-generation sourcing.
2. **Hybrid A25A-FXS parts (Camry Hybrid, VIN 4T1B31HK...)**: eCVT (P710) with Toyota WS-for-eCVT semantics, different coolant circuits (engine + inverter), H5/LN2 battery, no 8AT. This VIN 4T1B11HK is GAS — "inverter coolant" or eCVT anywhere = FAIL.
3. **0W-16 vs 0W-20**: correct answer is 0W-16 certified; 0W-20 is an explicitly permitted *fallback only*, with a mandatory 5,000-mi/6-mo interval and return to 0W-16 next change. "0W-20 recommended" (unqualified) = wrong; "0W-16 only, 0W-20 forbidden" = also wrong.
4. **V6 2GR-FKS decoys**: 0W-20 oil, ~5.7 qt capacity, and — from this very guide — spark plugs at **60,000 mi (2GR-FKS engine only)**. A pipeline reading the 60k page without the engine qualifier will emit a 60k plug interval for the I4; correct A25A interval is 120k.
5. **6-speed-era ATF semantics**: both gens use "Toyota WS" by name, so fluid-name match is NOT proof of correct variant — capacities (7.3 L total UB80E vs U760E figures) and the sealed no-dipstick level-check procedure differ. Toyota T-IV ATF = two generations wrong.
6. **Spark plug near-miss part numbers**: 90919-01297 / FC20HR-Q8 is the M20A-FKS 2.0L (Corolla/Corolla Cross) plug; 90919-01275 and 90919-01253 (SC20HR11) are other Toyota applications. Only 90919-01289 (FC16HR-Q8) is correct for 2018+ Camry 2.5.
7. **Battery 24F**: dominant forum/aftermarket answer from 25 years of prior Camrys; Toyota's own 2019 application chart says H6 for all non-hybrid 2019 Camrys.
8. **Look-wrong-but-correct values (do NOT "fix")**: cabin air filter every **10,000 mi** (verbatim in the official guide, though aftermarket sources say 15k–30k); **no scheduled brake-fluid or ATF replacement** under normal driving (US guide inspect-only); coolant first change deferred all the way to **100k mi**.
