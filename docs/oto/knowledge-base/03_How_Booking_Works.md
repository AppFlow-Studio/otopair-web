# How Booking Works

## Overview

In the Otopair app, a driver goes from "my car is doing something" to a booked slot in about 90 seconds. There's no service menu to decode: the driver tells Oto what the car is doing — or asks for a service by name — and every verified shop shows its own total for that exact car. A booking is a request the shop accepts, secured by a $20 hold.

## How it works, in seven steps

1. **Tell Oto what the car is doing** — by voice or by text, in your own words. No service menu, no form. Oto asks one narrowing question, not twenty
2. **Oto scopes the job** — it reads the car from its VIN (or year, make and model), checks the service history, the manufacturer's data for that exact car and any stored codes, and names the job a shop can price. A guide, not a diagnosis
3. **Every verified shop shows its total for your car** — each shop sets its own price, and you see the full amounts side by side. You choose the shop and the time yourself, and you can pick one of the shop's mechanics or let any available mechanic take it
4. **Book & Pay places a $20 hold** — an authorization, not a charge (Apple Pay, Google Pay or card). The booking goes to the shop to accept
5. **The shop inspects the car and confirms the estimate** — within what you approved, it confirms on its own. You follow along in the Bookings tab
6. **Anything extra needs your yes** — added work and its price come to you in the app, with 24 hours to answer. Declined work is never charged
7. **You're charged when the job is marked complete** — the receipt shows what happened to your hold and the parts that went in. Then you can review the shop, once

## After you book

- The shop accepts the booking request. If it doesn't — within 48 hours, or by 2 hours past the requested time, whichever comes first — the request expires, the $20 hold is released, nothing is charged, and the app invites you to rebook
- The booking shows up in the Bookings tab with its live status
- The app sends a reminder before the appointment (the shop sets how far ahead)

## On the day of service

- Bring the car to the shop at the time you booked — Otopair is not a mobile mechanic
- The shop inspects the car, confirms the price and does the work; the booking's status updates as the job moves
- When the shop marks the job complete, you're charged the confirmed price — never more than you approved — and an itemized receipt is issued
- The service goes onto your car's record

## Features that make booking faster

- **Re-Book** from a past booking pulls the same shop and service
- **Map or list** of nearby verified shops, sorted by closest, best rated or best price — or take Oto's pick
- Open times come straight from each shop's schedule

## Rescheduling and cancelling

- Reschedule from the booking — free up to 12 hours before the appointment, up to 2 times per booking; the shop re-confirms the new time. After that, message the shop
- Changing the service or the shop means cancelling and rebooking
- Cancel from the booking — free up to 24 hours before the appointment. Inside 24 hours the $20 hold is kept as a late-cancellation fee, never more than $20
- The booking shows the exact windows before you confirm. Every case is in "Cancellations, Disputes and Warranty"

## If the shop finds more

The total you approve when you book is a ceiling. If the shop finds more, it sends the added work and its price in the app, and you have 24 hours to answer. Declined work is never charged — the shop completes what you booked. The details are in "Pricing".

## How to answer common questions

**"How do I book?"**
"In the app: tell Oto what the car's doing, see each verified shop's total for your exact car, pick a shop and a time, and book with a $20 hold. The shop inspects the car and confirms the price, and you're charged when the job's done. It takes about 90 seconds — the app's launching soon, so join the launch list to hear first."

**"Is my appointment confirmed right away?"**
"Your booking goes to the shop to accept, with the $20 hold on your card. If the shop doesn't accept in time, the request expires, the hold is released and nothing is charged."

**"Can I cancel my booking?"**
"Yes, from the booking in the app. It's free up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours, the $20 hold is kept as a late-cancellation fee — never more than $20. The app shows you which applies before you confirm."

**"What if the price changes after I book?"**
"It can't go above what you approved without your yes in the app. If the shop finds more, you get a request with the price and 24 hours to answer — and declined work is never charged."

**"Can I pick my mechanic?"**
"In the app, yes — when you choose a shop you can pick one of its mechanics, or let any available mechanic take the job."

## Important boundaries

- Do **not** say a booking is confirmed the moment it's made — the shop accepts it first
- Do **not** say anything on the website books a real appointment — the walkthrough on screen is a sample
