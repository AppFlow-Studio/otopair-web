# Service Catalog

## Overview

Otopair launches with a focused catalog of **23 services across 7 categories** that cover the vast majority of independent shop work. Each service has a transparent, line-itemized price built specifically for the customer's car.

## Diagnostics

- **Diagnostic Scan** — Reads the vehicle's OBD-II system for stored and pending trouble codes. Triggered by symptoms, check engine lights, or as part of a comprehensive assessment
- **Check Engine Light** — Diagnose and address the underlying cause of an active check engine light

## Compliance

- **State Inspection** — NY annual safety inspection (state fee included separately on receipt)
- **Emissions Test** — NY emissions testing where applicable

## Routine Maintenance

- **Oil Change** — Engine oil and filter replacement using OEM-specified viscosity and capacity for the exact vehicle
- **Filter Replacement** — Air, cabin, and fuel filter replacements
- **Spark Plugs** — Vehicle-specific spark plug replacement at the OEM-recommended interval
- **Timing Belt** — Belt replacement (where applicable; chain-driven engines do not require this)
- **Coolant Flush** — Drain, flush, and refill with OEM-spec coolant
- **Transmission Service** — Fluid service for vehicles with serviceable transmissions

## Tires
*(See the separate "How Tire Quotes Work" doc — tires use a different flow than other services.)*

- **Tire Rotation** — Front-to-back rotation to even out wear
- **Tire Balance** — Wheel balancing to eliminate vibration
- **Wheel Alignment** — Adjustment of suspension angles to spec
- **Tire Replacement** — Full tire replacement via the quote flow

## Brakes

- **Brake Pad Replacement** — Front or rear pads, OEM or OEM-equivalent
- **Rotor Resurfacing** — Resurface where rotor thickness allows; replace where it doesn't
- **Brake Fluid Flush** — Replacement of brake fluid (brake fluid absorbs moisture over time and is a safety-critical service)

## Battery

- **Battery Test** — Diagnostic test of battery health and charging system
- **Battery Replacement** — Replacement battery sized to the vehicle

## Fluids

- **Power Steering Flush** — Where applicable (electric power steering systems don't need this)
- **Differential Service** — For vehicles with a serviceable differential
- **Fuel System Cleaning** — Cleaning of the fuel injection system

## Services coming after launch (not yet bookable)

Otopair is actively working on these and they are NOT in scope at launch:
- Mobile mechanics (a mechanic who comes to you)
- Mobile detailing
- Towing

## Services NOT on the platform

Otopair is intentionally focused at launch. The following are out of scope:
- Collision and body work
- Paint work
- Glass replacement
- EV-specific high-voltage work
- Major engine or transmission rebuilds
- Performance modifications

## How to answer "do you offer X?"

- If it's on the catalog above: confirm and name the category
- If it's a "coming soon" service: "That's on the roadmap. Mobile mechanics, detailing, and towing are in testing — keep an eye on the app"
- If it's out of scope: "That's not something we cover. Otopair is focused on standard maintenance and repair, not body work, paint, or major rebuilds. For that, you'd want a specialty shop"
- If the user describes a symptom and isn't sure which service applies: "Sounds like a diagnostic is the right first step. A real mechanic can confirm what's going on before any work happens. Want me to find you a shop?"
