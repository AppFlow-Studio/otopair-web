## Adversarial traps (wrong-answer decoys the pipeline may ingest)

1. **2.0T LTG contamination (2018–2020 Equinox only)**: oil **dexos1 5W-30** at **4.7 L / 5.0 qt** (TechLink Malibu/Camaro LTG rows), spark plug **GM 12681659**, and a **9-speed Hydra-Matic 9T50** — note the trap premise "8AT" is itself wrong: the Equinox 2.0T never had an 8-speed; it was the 9T50 (DEXRON-VI, drain/fill ~5.65 L). Any 5W-30 / 5.0-qt / 9-speed / 12681659 answer on a 2024 = wrong-engine bleed. Bonus catalog noise: gmpartsgiant shows a "2022 Chevrolet Equinox 2.0L" plug (55504354) even though no US 2022 Equinox had a 2.0T — do not let catalog rows resurrect dead engines.
2. **1.6L LH7 "Whisper Diesel" (2018–2019 only)**: dexos2/dexosD-class 5W-30 oil, **no spark plugs** (glow plugs), DEF/DPF service items, diesel-specific filters. Any glow-plug/DEF/dexosD row on a 2024 gas 1.5T = wrong-variant. Engine air filter 84390002 IS legitimately shared with the diesel, so the air filter matching a diesel listing is NOT evidence of contamination.
3. **4th-gen 2025 Equinox bleed**: still a "1.5T" but a **new RPO (LSD** per AMSOIL's 2025 lookup**)**, and the 6-speed is GONE — **CVT on FWD, 8-speed automatic on AWD** (CVT itself dropped again for 2027 per Car Guide). Dealer "Equinox maintenance schedule" pages already describe the new gen (e.g. sawyerchevy's "transmission fluid at 45,000 — 8-speed or CVT"). Any CVT fluid, 8-speed ATF, or LSD-engine part on a 2024 = next-gen bleed.
4. **0W-20 vs 5W-30 viscosity confusion**: **2024's official spec is dexos1 0W-20** (GM TechLink chart, rev 10-2024, verbatim). The prompt's premise "GM moved the 1.5T to 5W-30 in later years" did NOT verify for the 3rd-gen 1.5T — 5W-30 answers come from (a) the 2.0T LTG, (b) aggregator-DB errors (oil-capacity.com lists "5W-30, 4.2 qt" for 2024 — wrong), (c) GM's real 0W-20→5W-30 moves on OTHER engines (2.7L L3B trucks, 1.2L LIH, etc.), and (d) 2025-gen "5W-30 or 0W-20" dealer-blog hedging. An unqualified 5W-30 on a 2024 1.5T = FAIL.
5. **Look-wrong-but-correct: the dual oil capacity.** FWD = 4.2 qt but **AWD = 5.3 qt** (both verbatim in GM's own chart). A 5.3-qt answer is CORRECT for an AWD Premier — do not "fix" it to 4.2; conversely 4.2 on an AWD build is the error. Any single-capacity answer without a drivetrain qualifier is at best half-right.
6. **Equinox EV contamination (2024+ separate BEV model)**: Ultium battery crossover sharing the "Equinox" name and model year — no engine oil, no plugs, no 6T45, battery-loop coolant and reduction-gear fluid instead; own KBB/AutoZone pages exist for "2024 Equinox EV". Any high-voltage/one-pedal/reduction-gear content on this gas VIN = FAIL.
7. **Battery Group 48/H6 decoys**: common answer for other GM platforms (Malibu/Blazer variants); Equinox 3rd gen is **Group 47 (H5)** per GM's own accessory-battery listing. Also don't let the AGM-vs-flooded split change the group answer.
