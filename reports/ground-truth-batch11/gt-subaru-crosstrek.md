# Ground truth: 2025 Subaru Crosstrek Limited 2.5L FB25D (GU generation, US market)

Primary anchors: Subaru TSB 01-167-08R "Powertrain Fluids" **Revised 03/17/25** (NHTSA-hosted PDF MC-11016205, read in full — text extracted locally); parts.subaru.com OEM catalog listings for 2024/2025 Crosstrek; Subaru US media press kit (2024 Crosstrek Wilderness / 2.5L DI specs); dealer W&M-schedule transcriptions.

## Adversarial traps (wrong-answer decoys)

1. **2.0L FB20D (base/Premium) data on the Limited** — the Limited is the **2.5 FB25D (182 hp)**; base/Premium are FB20D (152 hp). FB20D oil capacity is slightly lower (~4.2 qt) and forum consensus keeps CVTF-II for pre-2021 2.0 fills — but plug (22401AA941), air filter (16546AA210), and oil filter (15208AA21A) are legitimately SHARED across both engines, so a part match alone doesn't prove correct engine ID. Horsepower/engine-code/oil-capacity are the discriminators.
2. **Wrong CVTF family** — five Subaru CVT fluids circulate: CVTF-II green (older TR580), **CVTF-III (correct: SOA427V2600/2610, 2021+ Crosstrek per TSB 01-167-08R rev 03/17/25)**, High Torque CVTF orange (TR690 turbo/H6), CVTF LV (Ascent/2.4T TR690), and ATF WS (BRZ + Crosstrek PHEV). A CVTF-II answer for a 2025 Crosstrek 2.5 is the highest-probability wrong answer because most pre-2021 Crosstrek content says CVTF-II. The 03/17/25 TSB revision is the arbiter.
3. **2nd-gen GT (2018-2023) Crosstrek contamination** — cabin filter **72880FL00A (WRONG, GT) vs 72880FN00A (GU)**; BCI Group 35/25 battery (WRONG) vs **LN2 EN-spec EFB 620 CCA** (2024+); older coolant-capacity figures. BUT beware over-correcting: engine air filter 16546AA210 and oil filter 15208AA21A genuinely span both generations — flag-not-fail those.
4. **Crosstrek Plug-in Hybrid contamination** — the discontinued (2019-2023) Crosstrek Hybrid PHEV uses **ATF WS (SOA427V1300)** in its TH-series e-CVT, has a high-voltage battery, and different service items. Any ATF-WS/e-CVT/hybrid-battery answer on a 2025 gas Crosstrek = wrong vehicle. (There is NO 2025 US Crosstrek Hybrid; the new strong-hybrid arrived MY2026.)
5. **Wilderness-trim bleed-over** — Wilderness shares the FB25D + TR580 and all fluids, but has a **transmission oil cooler, 4.11 final drive (vs 3.70), 3,500-lb tow rating**, and severe-service-leaning CVT guidance. Cooler/final-drive/tow specs presented for a Limited = trim mix-up; fluid/part answers, however, legitimately match.
6. **Impreza-shared parts that legitimately cross** — GU Crosstrek is an Impreza-platform car: engine air filter 16546AA210, cabin filter 72880FN00A, oil filter 15208AA21A, and spark plug 22401AA941 all genuinely fit 2024+ Impreza too. An "Impreza" source label on these rows is NOT an error. Forester is the false friend: same FB25D engine but a DIFFERENT air filter (16546AA16A) and (pre-2025) different cabin filter.
7. **Treating rear-diff gear oil as N/A** — symmetrical AWD: the rear differential is a real, serviceable 75W-90 GL-5 item (~0.9 qt), and the front diff has its own gear-oil sump separate from the CVT fluid. "FWD/no rear diff" or "CVT fluid covers the front diff" = FAIL.
8. **Oil-filter number direction reversal vs wave-1 Forester file** — for the 2019 Forester, 15208AA170 was the catalog truth and 15208AA21A was flagged as a spec-DB error; for the 2024-25 Crosstrek the roles flip: **15208AA21A is the current catalog number** (AA20A/AA170 superseded). An auditor reusing the Forester ground truth verbatim will mis-grade this row.
