# Vehicle Health Score

## Overview

Every vehicle on Otopair has a Vehicle Health Score from **0 to 100%**. It is a living snapshot of how well a car is being protected over time.

## What the score represents

Vehicle Health is **predictive, not reactive**. It answers the question: *"How well is this car being cared for right now?"* It does NOT answer "How bad is this owner?" The score reflects the vehicle, not the human.

## What influences the score

- Maintenance recency (how recently key services were completed)
- Mileage relative to service intervals
- Time-based care (fluids, inspections, brake fluid age)
- Known unresolved issues reported by the driver

## What does NOT influence the score

- App inactivity — the score is never reduced because a user hasn't opened the app
- Rewards tier or membership status

## Core principles

- **Predictive, not reactive** — surfaces what's coming up, not just what's overdue
- **Calm, not alarming** — no red panic states unless truly critical
- **Informational, not judgmental** — surfaces facts, never blames the owner
- **Always paired with clear next steps** — never just a score, always a "here's what to do"
- **No sudden drops without explanation** — every change is contextualized
- **Slow decay only** — the score moves at the speed of real wear, not engagement gimmicks

## What completing a service does

When the driver completes a recommended service, the score gains back the portion that was lost from that service being due. The app surfaces a clear **+X%** indicator on completion so the driver can see the connection between the service and the score.

## Language guidance

Always use calm, protective language:
- ✅ "Service recommended" not "Service overdue"
- ✅ "Protection declining" not "Vehicle at risk"
- ✅ "Overdue by 500 miles" not "YOU'RE OVERDUE!"
- ✅ "Here's what to do next" — pair every status with a concrete action

## How to answer common questions

**"What does my health score mean?"**
"It's a snapshot of how well your car is keeping up with the maintenance it actually needs, based on your driving and your car's age. The score gives you a number; the app gives you the next step to take. It's a guide, not a grade."

**"My score dropped — what happened?"**
"Time and miles. Some services have time-based intervals — brake fluid, coolant, things like that — and they catch up with every car. The app will show you exactly what's coming due. Tap into the detail to see what to do next."

**"Does ignoring the app hurt my score?"**
"No. The score reflects your car, not your app behavior. It only moves when there's a real change to what your car needs."

## Important boundaries

- Do **not** use alarmist language ("danger," "failure," "critical risk") unless the situation is genuinely safety-critical
- Do **not** describe the score as a "grade" or imply judgment
- Do **not** quote internal calculation modifiers (age tier, mileage tier, driving pattern multipliers, etc.) — those are internal mechanics
- Do **not** suggest the score is a competition or a leaderboard
- The right tone is **calm and protective**, never urgent or shaming
