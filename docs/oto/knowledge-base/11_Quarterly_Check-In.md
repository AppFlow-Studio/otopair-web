# Quarterly Check-In

## Overview

A quick check-in in the Otopair app keeps what the app knows about a car current: the mileage, any recent service, warning lights, and a few questions that depend on the car. Nothing is read from the car — the app has no way to see the odometer — so it asks. The answers keep maintenance recommendations and the Vehicle Health Score accurate as the car ages and the miles add up.

## Why quarterly

Services happen outside the app. Symptoms appear. Driving patterns shift. If the app only knew what it learned when the car was added, its recommendations would drift further from reality every week. **Every 90 days** lines up with natural ownership rhythms — seasonal tire changes, inspection cycles, oil change intervals.

## When it happens

- **Before a car's first booking.** The app asks the first check-in before that car can be booked
- **Then every 90 days**, counted from the last check-in. When one is due, a banner shows on the car in the app's **Cars** tab
- **No push notification**, and **no badge** on the app icon

## What it asks

- The mileage on the odometer
- Whether any service was done on the car recently, and whether it was through Otopair
- Whether any warning lights are on, or anything feels off
- A few more that depend on the car — for example whether it starts without hesitation, whether a lease is ending soon, or whether the driving has changed

The app says it takes about a minute. Answers are self-reported; a record from a shop is what makes an item verified.

## What completion feels like

- Calm confirmation: *"All set — your [car name] is up to date."*
- No confetti, no "great job," no gamification

## How to answer common questions

**"Do I have to do the quarterly check-in?"**
"The first one comes before a car's first booking — the app asks it before that car can be booked. After that, a banner on your car in the app lets you know when the next one's due, every 90 days. It takes about a minute."

**"What's in it?"**
"Quick questions about your car — the mileage, any recent service, any warning lights — plus a few that depend on the car. About a minute."

**"Will it keep pinging me?"**
"No — there's no push notification and no badge. When one's due, it shows up as a banner on your car in the app."

## Important boundaries

- Do **not** give a number of questions — it depends on the car
- Do **not** say it takes 30 seconds, that the first check-in can be skipped before a first booking, or that skipping one marks the score as an estimate
- Do **not** explain the internal mode-classification logic or how answers change interval calculations
- The right tone is "a friend asking how the car is" — not a form, not a survey
