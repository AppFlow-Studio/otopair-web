# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 20, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 20, 2026

Otopair ("Otopair," "we," "us," or "our") provides an online platform that connects vehicle owners with automotive repair shops and supports shop operations. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Otopair, you agree to the practices described below.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, do not use Otopair.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account & Identity Information
When you register or are invited to Otopair, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Mobile phone number
- Password credentials (managed by our authentication provider, Clerk)
- Profile photo (optional)
- Role (vehicle owner, shop owner, mechanic, administrator)
- Shop affiliation (for shop owners and mechanics)

### 1.2 Vehicle Data
To deliver our core service, we collect and store information about your vehicles, including:

- Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
- Make, model, year, trim
- License plate (where provided)
- Mileage / odometer readings
- Tire specifications (size, brand, DOT codes, tread depth, pressure history)
- Service history, repair records, and prior diagnostic results
- Photos of the vehicle and its components uploaded by you or by an authorized shop
- Vehicle passport records associated with your account

This data may be entered by you, imported from third-party data providers, decoded from a VIN, or submitted on your behalf by a shop you have authorized.

### 1.3 Booking & Transaction Data
- Appointment dates, times, services requested, and notes
- Shop assignments, mechanic assignments, and bay/lane assignments
- Booking status (pending, confirmed, in progress, completed, cancelled, no-show)
- Invoices, estimates, line items, and payment status

### 1.4 Communications Data
- **SMS messages**: We send and log transactional SMS messages (booking confirmations, reminders, status updates, walk-in claim links, one-time codes). We retain message content, delivery status, timestamps, and the recipient phone number.
- **Email**: We send and log transactional emails (account verification, booking notifications, receipts, marketing where you have opted in). We retain message content, delivery status, opens/clicks where the email provider reports them, and the recipient address.
- **In-app messages**: Notes, comments, and messages exchanged between vehicle owners, shops, and Otopair staff.

### 1.5 Device & Usage Data
- IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
- Pages visited, features used, and timestamps
- Referring URLs and exit URLs
- Crash reports and diagnostic logs
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 7)

### 1.6 Location Data
Approximate location may be inferred from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location unless you explicitly grant permission within a future feature.

### 1.7 Information from Third Parties
- **Authentication provider (Clerk)**: identity and session data
- **VIN decoders and OEM data providers**: vehicle specifications
- **Payment processors**: payment status (we do not store full card numbers)
- **Shops**: information they add about your vehicle or service history

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and maintain your account
- Deliver, schedule, and confirm vehicle service appointments
- Send SMS and email notifications related to your bookings and account
- Provide vehicle passports and service history to authorized shops and vehicle owners
- Pre-fill repair orders and parts lookups (e.g., tire specifications)
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service
- Improve and develop new features
- Send marketing communications **only where you have opted in** (you may opt out at any time)

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 3. SMS Messaging — Specific Disclosures

By providing your mobile phone number, you consent to receive SMS messages from Otopair related to your account and bookings, including but not limited to:

- Appointment confirmations and reminders
- Status updates ("vehicle ready for pickup," "mechanic assigned," etc.)
- Walk-in claim links allowing you to claim a record created by a shop on your behalf
- One-time verification codes
- Customer service replies

**Frequency:** Message frequency varies based on your activity.
**Cost:** Standard message and data rates may apply from your carrier.
**Opt-out:** Reply **STOP** to any SMS message to unsubscribe from non-essential SMS. You may continue to receive transactional security messages required to operate your account. Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact us at the address below.
**Carriers:** Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We use third-party SMS providers to deliver these messages. The provider may process your phone number and message metadata on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

---

## 4. Email Messaging

Transactional emails (account, security, booking, billing) are sent regardless of marketing preferences because they are required for service operation. Marketing emails are sent only with your consent and include an unsubscribe link in every message. We use a third-party email provider (Resend) to deliver email on our behalf.

## 5. How We Share Information

We share information only as described below.

### 5.1 With Shops You Engage
When you book or are claimed by a shop, that shop receives the information necessary to service your vehicle, including your name, contact details, vehicle data, and service history relevant to the booking.

### 5.2 With Vehicle Owners (for Shops)
Shops share repair details, photos, estimates, and history back to the vehicle owner through their Otopair account.

### 5.3 With Service Providers
We share information with vendors who help us operate the platform, including:

- Authentication (Clerk)
- Backend infrastructure (Convex)
- SMS delivery providers
- Email delivery providers (Resend)
- Payment processors
- Analytics and error monitoring
- Cloud hosting

These providers are contractually limited to using your information only to perform services for us.

### 5.4 For Legal Reasons
We may disclose information to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect our rights, or investigate fraud or abuse.

### 5.5 Business Transfers
If Otopair is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred subject to this Privacy Policy.

### 5.6 With Your Consent
We share information for any other purpose only with your consent.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and as required to:

- Provide ongoing services
- Maintain vehicle service history for warranty, safety, and recall purposes
- Comply with legal, tax, and accounting requirements
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Vehicle service records may be retained on a vehicle's passport even after individual user accounts are closed, to preserve the integrity of the vehicle's history for subsequent owners. Personally identifying information attached to those records may be removed or anonymized upon request, subject to legal hold requirements.

You may request deletion as described in Section 9.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Keep you signed in
- Remember preferences
- Measure usage and performance
- Detect fraud

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may impair core functionality such as login.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure; you use Otopair at your own risk and should choose a strong, unique password.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Receive a portable copy of your information
- Withdraw consent for marketing communications
- Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell personal information)

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 13. We will verify your identity before responding and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Otopair is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can delete it.
