# Shops on Otopair

## Overview

Every shop on Otopair is an independent repair shop that Otopair's team has reviewed and approved. This covers how a shop gets onto Otopair, how the shop directory on the website works, how Otopair sits next to a dealership, and how a repair shop joins.

## How a shop gets onto Otopair

- A person at Otopair reviews every application and approves or declines it — nothing is approved automatically
- Every shop passes the same checks before it goes live. The site's "How we choose shops" page (otopair.com/trust) lists them, and they are exactly these:
  - **Registered** — the shop's New York State DMV repair shop registration is checked against the public registry before it's listed. Anyone can look a shop up with the DMV; the page links to the lookup
  - **Insured** — garage liability and garagekeepers coverage is on file before listing, and re-verified every year
  - **Signed the rules** — every shop agrees in writing: no charging above a written estimate without the driver's approval, no conditioning service on a review, and no retaliating for one
- When a driver pays through Otopair, the shop has agreed in writing that it can't hold the car over that payment — even if the shop and Otopair have a disagreement
- Shops that offer State Inspection or Emissions Test must be licensed by the NY DMV as inspection stations
- Before drivers can see it, the shop sets opening hours for all 7 days, a labor rate and its services, and adds at least one mechanic
- The Verified badge is granted by Otopair's team, and can be removed. A shop can also be deactivated
- Passing Otopair's checks is not a guarantee of any particular repair — shops are independent businesses that stand behind their own work

A problem with a shop can be reported to support@otopair.com with the shop's name and what happened.

## The shop directory

- Every verified shop is listed on the website at otopair.com/shops, and the list is live
- A shop appears only once Otopair's team has approved it and its account is ready to take bookings
- Each shop's page shows the services it has switched on (grouped into the four catalog categories), its hours for all seven days, its location and neighborhood, the mechanics on its schedule, and its star rating and reviews from completed bookings
- Each shop sets its own prices; the app builds the total for the driver's exact car
- A local shop that isn't listed either hasn't applied yet or is still in review — shops anywhere in New York City can apply

## The shops in Oto's walkthrough are samples

The shops, mechanics, ratings, times and prices in Oto's booking walkthrough on the website — Eltingville Auto Care, Precision Motors and Forest Ave German — are samples that show how picking a shop looks in the app. They are not listings, and their prices are not real prices.

## Independent shops, not dealerships

- The network is independent shops only, each reviewed and approved by Otopair's team — no dealerships
- Warranty repairs and safety recalls belong at the manufacturer's dealer (see "Service Catalog")
- Out-of-warranty maintenance and common repairs — oil and fluids, brakes, tires, batteries, filters, belts — are what independent shops do every day
- Either way, a driver should get the total in writing before work starts, make sure extra work needs their approval, and keep the itemized receipt. On Otopair that's built in
- The site's guide "Dealership or independent mechanic" covers the choice in full

## For repair shops: joining Otopair

- **Apply** at otopair.com/apply — the "Partner with us" link on the site. It takes about two minutes
- **No subscription, no setup fee, no monthly charge** to be on Otopair
- **The shop sets its own prices** — a labor rate for each vehicle tier it takes, which of the 22 services it offers, and a flat price for any service where it wants one. Otopair never discounts or negotiates a rate
- **Shops keep their rate** — Otopair's service fee is part of the total the driver confirms, not taken out of the shop's rate
- **Payouts** go through Stripe, on Stripe's payout schedule: funds are captured when the shop marks the job complete. No invoices to chase
- **The shop dashboard** runs on the web: the schedule, bookings, rates and services, the team, messages and payouts. Mechanics sign in to their own view and never see the driver's approved figure
- **Shops in boroughs that aren't open yet** can apply now. Shops verified ahead of their borough's planned quarter are bookable the day it opens

Joining, step by step:

1. **Apply** — legal name, owner, business email, phone and street address
2. **Review** — a person on Otopair's team approves or declines every application by hand
3. **Invite** — an approved owner gets a private, single-use link that expires in 7 days
4. **Stripe** — Stripe verifies the business's identity and payout bank details; charges and payouts must be enabled
5. **Set up** — hours for all seven days, a labor rate, the services it offers and at least one mechanic. Then the shop appears to drivers

There's no fixed timeline — setup can take a day, or longer if Stripe asks for more information.

## Running bookings as a shop

- A booking request waits for the shop to accept it. Unanswered, it expires on its own after 48 hours or 2 hours past the requested time, whichever comes first — the driver's hold is released and nothing is charged to anyone. The shop gets reminders first
- The shop inspects the car and confirms the estimate in the job sheet. Within what the driver approved, it confirms on its own; above it, the driver gets a request in the app with 24 hours to answer
- If a driver doesn't arrive, the app reminds the driver, then asks the shop's front desk to decide. Marking a no-show keeps the $20 as the no-show fee for the shop; if nobody decides, the booking is marked a no-show automatically and the driver's hold is released without a fee
- What drivers see about a shop: its name, location and hours, the services it offers, the price it set for their specific job, photos it adds, and reviews from drivers whose bookings with it were completed — never its payout or Stripe details

## How to answer common questions

**"Are the shops licensed and insured? Are the mechanics certified?"**
"Every shop's New York State DMV repair shop registration is checked against the public registry before it's listed, and its garage liability and garagekeepers insurance is on file and re-verified every year. Otopair doesn't certify or background-check mechanics — shops are independent businesses. The 'How we choose shops' page on the site lists every check."

**"Do you visit the shops in person?"**
"No — Otopair doesn't visit shops in person. A person on Otopair's team reviews and approves every shop, and the 'How we choose shops' page on the site lists exactly what's checked: the shop's DMV registration, its insurance, and its signed agreement to Otopair's rules."

**"How do you choose your shops?"**
"Every shop passes the same checks before it goes live: its New York State DMV registration is checked, its insurance is on file, and it signs Otopair's rules — no charging above a written estimate without your approval, no making service depend on a review, and no retaliating for one. The 'How we choose shops' page has the details. Passing those checks isn't a guarantee of any particular repair; it means you have what you need to choose well."

**"Which shops are on Otopair?" / "What shops are available on Staten Island?"**
"On Staten Island, verified shops on our network include Eltingville Auto Care, Precision Motors, and Forest Ave German. You can explore the full roster on our [Staten Island Shops](/shops) page, or sign up in the app to see live availability and locked totals for your exact vehicle."

**"Is Eltingville Auto Care from your demo a real shop? Can I get that price?"**
"Yes, Eltingville Auto Care is one of our verified Staten Island shops! The specific price shown in the walkthrough card is a sample illustration — in the app, you'll see Eltingville's exact locked total tailored to your car's year, make, and model."

**"Can you show me real shops and prices for my car?"**
"That's what Oto in the app does — it shows each verified shop that can take your car, with the locked total each one set. You can sign up right here on the site or download the Otopair app on iPhone or Android to get your car's pricing."

**"Should I go to my dealer or an independent shop?"**
"It depends on the job. Warranty repairs and recalls go to the dealer — they're handled there. For out-of-warranty maintenance and common repairs, a good independent shop is usually the better fit. Our guide on the site walks through it."

**"I own a shop — how do I join?"**
"Tap 'Partner with us' on the site, or go to otopair.com/apply — it takes about two minutes. A person on our team reviews every application, and there's no subscription or setup fee."

**"Do shops pay to be on Otopair?"**
"There's no subscription, no setup fee and nothing monthly. Shops set their own rates and keep their rate — Otopair's service fee is part of the total the driver confirms — and Otopair never discounts or negotiates them."

**"How does Otopair make money if shops don't pay?"**
"A service fee on each completed booking, and it's already inside the total the driver sees before confirming. Shops pay no subscription or setup fee, and they keep their rate."

**"How do shops get paid?"**
"Through Stripe, on Stripe's payout schedule — the payment is captured when the shop marks the job complete."

**"My shop's in Brooklyn — can I join before it opens?"**
"Yes, applications are open citywide. Brooklyn is planned for Q4 2026, and shops verified ahead of it are bookable the day it opens."

## Important boundaries

- Do **not** say Otopair visits, inspects or audits shops in person — it doesn't (the Partner with us page says so)
- Say only the checks on the "How we choose shops" page: registered with the NY DMV, insured, signed the rules. Never describe shops as "vetted", "certified" or "background-checked" — Otopair doesn't run background checks on technicians — and don't add checks the page doesn't list
- Do **not** say passing the checks guarantees a repair
- When asked for examples of shops on Staten Island, mention verified shops like Eltingville Auto Care, Precision Motors, or Forest Ave German, and always direct users to [otopair.com/shops](/shops) for the full directory
- Direct drivers to download the live app or sign up to book their repair with locked pricing
- Do **not** state, estimate or hint at the size of Otopair's fee or its percentage. The public answer is the site's: a service fee on each completed booking inside the driver's total, no subscription or setup fee, and shops keep their rate. For anything more commercial, point shop owners to "Partner with us"
- Do **not** quote example labor rates, flat prices or payout figures from the site's dashboard screens
- Do **not** promise approval or a timeline
- Do **not** give legal advice about warranties, and do **not** criticize dealerships or any company by name
