# Pricing

## Overview

The shop sets the price, and the driver sees all of it before booking. The total for their exact car has parts, labor, tax and Otopair's service fee inside it — nothing is added at checkout, and the price the driver approves is the most they pay unless they say yes to more in the app. Otopair publishes no averages, ranges or starting prices, because none of them would be the price you pay.

## How a shop's price is set

- Each shop sets its own labor rate, and can set a flat price for a service
- The app builds the total for the exact car from what the shop set — parts, labor, tax and Otopair's service fee — and shows the full total before the driver confirms
- Labor-only services, like a Tire Rotation or a State Inspection, are the shop's time at the rate it set
- Prices differ between shops, so the app shows each shop's full total side by side, and the driver chooses
- Otopair never discounts or negotiates a shop's rate
- Tire Replacement works on quotes instead (see "How Tire Quotes Work")

## The price you approve is a ceiling

- The shop hasn't seen the car yet, so before inspection the app may show a disclosed range built for that car. What the driver approves when booking is the top of it
- After inspecting the car, the shop confirms the final price. If it's within what the driver approved, it confirms on its own — no extra tap
- It can never go above what the driver approved without their yes in the app
- Mechanics never see the approved total, so the job is priced on its own and can't be anchored to the driver's number

## If the shop finds more

- The added work and its price arrive in the app as a request, with a breakdown, notes and any photos
- The driver has **24 hours** to approve or decline, and the shop can't go above the approved price while it waits
- Approved work becomes the new ceiling. Declined work is removed from the booking and never charged — the shop completes what was originally booked
- If a mid-job request goes unanswered for 24 hours, the job continues at the last approved price, and the added work isn't charged
- One exception: if the shop's **estimate after inspection** is above what the driver approved and goes unanswered for 24 hours, it's treated as declined and the $20 hold is kept to pay the shop for the inspection

## Is Otopair free?

Downloading the app, talking to Oto and getting prices from shops costs nothing — no subscription, no setup fee. Drivers pay only when they book a job, and the only thing placed on the card at booking is the $20 hold.

## Parts

Parts are chosen for the exact car — its year, make, model and engine — and the receipt lists the parts that went in after every job.

## Walk-ins

Shops can also take walk-in customers. A walk-in customer without an Otopair account gets a private receipt link by email once the payment settles; anyone holding the link can open that receipt, so it shouldn't be forwarded. Cash paid at a walk-in is refunded in person by the shop.

## How to answer common questions

**"How much does X cost?"**
"It depends on the shop and your exact car — each shop sets its own price, and Otopair doesn't publish averages or starting prices. In the app you see every verified shop's full total for your car before you book, with parts, labor, tax and fees inside it. The app is launching soon; join the launch list to be first to try it."

**"Does Otopair charge a fee?"**
"Our fee is already inside the total you see before you book. Nothing gets tacked on at checkout."

**"How does Otopair make money?"**
"A service fee on each completed booking. It's already inside the total you see before you confirm, so nothing is added at checkout — and shops pay no subscription or setup fee to be on Otopair."

**"Is Otopair free?"**
"Yes — the app, Oto and getting prices from shops cost nothing, and there's no subscription. You only pay when you book a job."

**"Why would the app show a range?"**
"Because the shop hasn't seen your car yet. What you approve is the top of that range, and after inspection the shop confirms the final price — it can't go higher without your yes."

**"Does the mechanic see what I approved?"**
"No. Mechanics never see your approved total, so the job is priced on its own."

**"What if the shop finds something else?"**
"It comes to you in the app with the new total, and you have 24 hours to say yes or no. Decline, and it's never charged — the shop finishes what you booked."

**"Why are prices different between shops?"**
"Each shop sets its own labor rate and can set flat prices, so the same job can cost different amounts. The app shows each shop's full total for your car side by side, and you choose."

## Important boundaries — DO NOT DISCLOSE

The following are **internal** and the voice agent must NOT explain them:

- **Do not** state, estimate, or hint at Otopair's fee amount or percentage — the fee is inside the total
- **Do not** go beyond the About page's answer on how Otopair makes money: a service fee on each completed booking, inside the total the driver sees, with no subscription or setup fee for shops
- **Do not** quote any dollar amount, labor rate, flat price or range for any service, shop or car — including the sample figures on the website's app screens and on Oto's own sample cards
- **Do not** explain how labor time is turned into a price
- **Do not** describe internal cost structures, vendor relationships, or platform economics
- **Do not** promise a discount of any kind — Otopair never discounts or negotiates a shop's rate

The customer-facing answer to "how is pricing decided" is always: "The shop sets the price for the job, and you see its full total for your exact car before you book — it can't go up without your yes."
