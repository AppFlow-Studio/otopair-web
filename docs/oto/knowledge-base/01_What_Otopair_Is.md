# What Otopair Is

## Overview

Otopair is a trust-first car repair marketplace for New York City. It sits between drivers who need a repair and independent shops that Otopair's team has reviewed and approved. In the Otopair app, a driver tells Oto what the car is doing, Oto turns that into a job a shop can price, and every verified shop nearby shows the full total it set for that exact car. The driver picks the shop and the time, books with a $20 hold, and the price they approve is the most they pay — extra work needs their yes in the app before it happens.

Otopair is not a garage. The shop does the repair, sets its own prices and stands behind its work. Otopair verifies the shop, scopes the job, locks the price, settles the payment through Stripe and keeps the record of what was done.

## Core facts

- Available on iPhone and Android — the app is live and onboarding users; drivers download it to get locked pricing and book
- Free to download and use — no subscription; drivers pay only when they book a job
- Drivers book in the app — repair shops use a separate web dashboard
- Live first in Staten Island, NYC, then the rest of the city one borough at a time (see "Where Otopair Works")
- Independent shops only — every shop is reviewed and approved by Otopair's team before drivers can book it (see "Shops on Otopair")
- One-way reviews: drivers review the shop, and optionally the mechanic, only after a completed booking
- The full total for your exact car before you book — parts, labor, tax and fees inside it — and it can't go up without your yes

## What Otopair exists to solve

Otopair exists to remove fear, uncertainty, and distrust from car ownership. People do not hate maintaining their cars. They hate not knowing who to trust, not knowing if they are being overcharged, and feeling judged for not understanding cars. Otopair's job is to earn trust deeply enough that booking a service feels obvious.

## The product philosophy

Every Otopair feature is held to one question: does this make the user feel more confident and protected as a car owner? Otopair does not use dark patterns, countdown timers, scarcity language, or fake urgency. It is built to feel like a dashboard protecting an asset, not a storefront selling things.

## How it's different from other repair platforms

- Independent shops only, not chains or dealerships
- Every shop is reviewed and approved by hand before it goes live
- Each shop's full total for your exact car is shown before you book, and it's a ceiling — anything above it needs your yes in the app
- No upselling, no pressure, no fake urgency
- Every review comes from a completed Otopair booking

## What Otopair is not

- **Not a garage** — the shop does the repair and stands behind it
- **Not a price list** — Otopair publishes no averages, ranges or starting prices; every total is built for the exact car in the app
- **Not a mobile mechanic, a tow service or a dealership network** — see "Service Catalog"

## The vehicle data behind Otopair

Otopair's vehicle data — maintenance specs, service intervals, exact-fit parts and real-world labor times — is what makes a price for an exact car possible. Every value carries its source, in four layers:

- **Official** — owner's manuals and manufacturer data
- **Researched** — Otopair's own research, checked against the car's specification
- **Measured** — timings and parts read from completed Otopair jobs
- **Confirmed** — a mechanic or the Otopair team verified it by hand

A value the system can't stand behind is held back rather than shown. The catalog is published as its own product, **OtoIndex**, open to developers through an API; the site's About page links to it.

**"Where does your car data come from?"**
"Every value carries its source: owner's manuals and manufacturer data, Otopair's own research checked against the car's spec, what shops measure on completed Otopair jobs, and values a mechanic or our team confirmed by hand. If the system can't stand behind a value, it's held back rather than shown."

**"Do you have an API for car data?"**
"Yes — the catalog is published as its own product, OtoIndex, for developers. The About page on otopair.com links to it."

- Do **not** name data vendors, describe how the data is built or how labor time becomes a price, or give OtoIndex a web address
