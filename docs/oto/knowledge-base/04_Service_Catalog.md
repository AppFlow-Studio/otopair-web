# Service Catalog

## Overview

Otopair's catalog has **22 bookable services in 4 categories** — Routine, Tires & Brakes, Scheduled Service and Inspections — the same four lists the app shows. Each shop chooses which of the 22 it offers and sets its own price, built for the customer's exact car. Oto in the app shows only the services that apply to the driver's car, so their own list may be shorter. Always use the exact catalog names below: the name the driver books is the name the shop sees on its board.

## How to read each entry

- **Parts included** — the parts or fluids the job needs are priced for the exact car and included in the total. **Labor only** — no parts are sold with it; the price is the shop's time at the labor rate it set
- **Applies to** — which cars the app offers it for
- **When it's due** — the usual cue. The car's own schedule decides the interval, and a warning light is a reason to get the car checked, not proof a particular service is needed
- **Typical time** — what the app's service list shows. It's a typical figure, not a promise for a specific shop or car

## Routine

- **Oil Change** — Engine oil and filter replacement with OEM-spec oil. Parts included. Applies to gas and hybrid engines. Due when the car's service schedule calls for it. The app lists about 24 min
- **Filter Replacement** — Engine air filter and cabin air filter replacement. Parts included. Applies to any car Otopair can identify. Due when the car's maintenance schedule calls for it — these are interval items, not symptom items. The app lists about 21 min
- **Battery Replacement** — A new battery matched to the car's group size and CCA (cold-cranking amps) rating. Parts included. Applies to any car. Due when a Battery Test says the battery is done; a battery light or slow starting is the cue to test it first. The app lists about 30 min

## Tires & Brakes

- **Tire Rotation** — Rotating the tires to promote even wear and extend tire life. Labor only. Applies only where the car's tire setup allows rotation. Due when the car's schedule calls for it. The app lists about 24 min
- **Tire Balance** — Balancing all four wheels to eliminate vibration. Labor only. Applies to any car. Due when you feel vibration through the wheel or seat at speed, or with new tires. The app lists about 45 min
- **Wheel Alignment** — Adjusting the wheel angles to the manufacturer's specification. Labor only. Applies to any car. Due when the car pulls to one side or the steering wheel sits off-center, after a hard pothole or curb hit, with new tires, and on schedule. The app lists about 1 hr
- **Tire Replacement** — Mounting and balancing new tires to the car's OEM size specification. Parts included. Applies to any car. Due when a tire is worn or damaged. The only tire service booked as a quote request: shops quote the exact tire, so the app shows "Quote" instead of a time (see "How Tire Quotes Work")
- **Brake Pad Replacement** — Replacing the front and/or rear brake pads with OEM parts; the shop confirms which axle after inspecting. Parts included. Applies to any car. A squeal or a grind when braking is the usual cue. The app lists about 1 hr 30 min
- **Rotor Replacement** — Replacing the brake rotors and pads together, on the front and/or rear axle. Parts included. Applies to any car. The usual cue is braking that pulses, or pads worn into the rotors. There is no separate rotor resurfacing service. The app lists about 3 hr
- **Brake Fluid Flush** — A full brake fluid flush and bleed at all four corners. Fluid included. Applies to any car. Due when the car's schedule calls for it — brake fluid ages whether you drive or not. The app lists about 51 min

## Scheduled Service

- **Spark Plugs** — Spark plug replacement with OEM-spec plugs. Parts included. Applies to gas and hybrid engines. Due when the car's service schedule calls for it. The app lists about 1 hr 30 min
- **Timing Belt** — Timing belt kit replacement: the belt, tensioner and idler pulleys. Parts included. Belt-driven engines only (a chain-driven engine has no belt to replace). Due strictly on the car's schedule — it's an interval item, not a symptom item. The app lists about 5 hr
- **Coolant Flush** — A full cooling system flush and refill with OEM coolant. Fluid included. Applies to any car. Due when the car's schedule calls for a cooling-system service. The app lists about 1 hr 15 min
- **Transmission Service** — A transmission fluid drain-and-fill with OEM-spec fluid. Fluid included. Applies to any car the app can identify; some transmissions are sealed, so the car's own schedule decides. The app lists about 1 hr 30 min
- **Power Steering Flush** — Flushing and replacing the power steering fluid. Fluid included. Hydraulic power steering only — most modern cars steer electrically, with no fluid to flush. Due when the car's schedule calls for it. The app lists about 45 min
- **Differential Service** — Draining and refilling the differential and transfer case fluid. Fluid included. AWD and RWD cars with a separate differential only (a front-wheel-drive car's final drive shares the transmission fluid). Due when the car's schedule calls for it. The app lists about 1 hr
- **Fuel System Cleaning** — Cleaning the fuel injectors and intake valves to restore performance. Parts included. Applies to gas and hybrid engines. Due when the car's schedule calls for it, or when performance has fallen off. The app lists about 1 hr

## Inspections

- **Diagnostic Scan** — An OBD-II scan that reads and clears the car's stored trouble codes, so the next step is a decision, not a guess. Labor only. 1996 and newer cars (OBD-II). Useful when a warning light is on and you want to know what the car is reporting before committing to a repair. The app lists about 30 min
- **Check Engine Light Diagnosis** — Diagnosing the check engine light: reading the code and finding the root cause, so you know what to book next. The repair itself is booked and approved separately. Labor only. 1996 and newer cars (OBD-II). Due when the check engine light comes on — a flashing light means stop driving (see "Warning Lights and Safety"). The app lists about 1 hr
- **State Inspection** — New York's annual state safety inspection certification. Labor only. Applies to any car; performed only by shops licensed by the NY DMV as inspection stations. Due once a year — book it before the expiry month on the current sticker ends. The app lists about 30 min
- **Emissions Test** — New York's state-required emissions compliance test. Labor only. Gas and hybrid engines; performed only by NY DMV-licensed inspection stations. Due when New York requires it for the car's registration. The app lists about 18 min
- **Battery Test** — A load test of the battery and a check of the charging system. Labor only. Applies to any car. Useful when the battery light comes on, the car is slow to start, before paying to replace a battery, and before winter. The app lists about 12 min

## What Otopair does not do

- **No mobile mechanics** — Otopair is a marketplace for booking a shop. The driver brings the car to the shop they booked, at the time they booked. Nobody is dispatched to a driveway
- **No towing, jump-starts or roadside help** — Otopair can't send anyone to a stranded car
- **No warranty repairs or safety recalls** — those belong at the manufacturer's dealer. Otopair is independent shops, for the out-of-warranty work that is most of a car's life
- **No recall lookups** — the app can't check a car for recalls
- None of these is announced as coming

## Not in the catalog

The catalog is focused on maintenance and common repairs. These aren't among the 22:
- Collision and body work
- Paint work
- Glass replacement
- EV-specific high-voltage work
- Major engine or transmission rebuilds
- Performance modifications

## How to answer "do you offer X?"

- If it's in the catalog: say it's one of the catalog's services, with its exact name and category, and show the service card. Don't say it's bookable now or near them — the app is in pre-launch, and each shop chooses which services it offers; the service's page on otopair.com shows which Staten Island shops list it
- If it's a mobile mechanic, towing or roadside help: "Otopair doesn't do that — you bring the car to the shop you book, and we can't send anyone out. If you're stuck right now, call a tow or roadside service."
- If it's recall or warranty work: "That belongs at your manufacturer's dealer — recall repairs are done there at no charge. Otopair is independent shops, for out-of-warranty work."
- If it's out of scope: "That's not something we cover. Otopair is focused on maintenance and common repairs, not body work, paint or major rebuilds — a specialty shop is the right call for that."
- If the visitor describes a symptom and isn't sure which service applies: "In the app, you'd describe it to Oto and it scopes the job a shop can price — the mechanic confirms what the car actually needs before any work. If the cause isn't clear, that can start with a Diagnostic Scan or a Check Engine Light Diagnosis. Want to see how booking works?"
- A warning light is rarely fixed by one specific service — never say a light "means" a particular service. Give the safe first step, then the possibilities (see "Warning Lights and Safety")

**"Can I book [service]?"**
"[Service] is in the Otopair catalog, under [category]. Each shop chooses which services it offers, and the [service] page on otopair.com shows the Staten Island shops that list it. You'll book it in the Otopair app, which is launching soon — join the launch list to get it first."

**"How long does [service] take?"**
"The app lists [service] at about [typical time]. That's a typical figure — your car and the shop's schedule decide the real time."

**"Can Otopair check my car for recalls?"**
"No — the app can't look up recalls. You can check your VIN on NHTSA's recall lookup, and recall repairs are done at no charge at your manufacturer's dealer."

## Important boundaries

- Always use the exact catalog names; never invent a service or rename one
- Do **not** attach a price to any service
- Typical times are only what the app's list shows — never promise how long a job will take at a specific shop, and never connect a time to a price
- Do **not** say which or how many shops offer a service — each shop chooses, and the service pages on the site show the live list
- Do **not** say a service is "definitely" bookable, or bookable now or today — the app is in pre-launch
