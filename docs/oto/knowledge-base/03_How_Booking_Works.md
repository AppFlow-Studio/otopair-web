# How Booking Works

## Overview

Otopair takes a driver from "my car needs something" to a confirmed appointment in six taps or fewer. The flow is the same whether the driver is doing routine maintenance, replacing tires, or asking about a brake job — about 40 seconds end to end.

## The six steps

1. **Pick your vehicle** — Choose from the vehicles already on your account, or add a new one
2. **Pick your service** — Choose from the catalog (oil change, brake pads, state inspection, diagnostic, tires, and so on). If you're not sure what's wrong, the in-app Oto AI assistant can ask a few questions and suggest a starting point — usually a diagnostic so a real mechanic can confirm before any work is done
3. **Find a mechanic on the map** — Browse nearby shops with profiles, ratings, real-time slot availability, and named mechanics. Pick a specific mechanic, or pick the shop and book the next available bay. The choice is yours
4. **Pick a time** — Open slots are shown in real time
5. **Review the details** — What you're booking and the full locked price for your car, with labor, parts, and taxes broken out — nothing is added later
6. **Confirm** — A $20 hold secures the appointment through the secure checkout (Apple Pay, Google Pay on Android, or card) — a hold, not a charge

## What happens after you confirm

- A confirmation screen appears with a checkmark and a light celebratory animation
- You get an **"Add to Calendar"** button to add the appointment to your phone
- You get an automatic reminder push notification **1 hour before** your appointment
- A **PDF receipt** is generated and stored under your service history
- The booking shows up in the **Upcoming** tab inside Bookings

## On the day of service

- Drop your car at the shop at the scheduled time
- The mechanic confirms vehicle details, does the work, and updates the status in real time
- You can follow along through the **Live Tracker** tab inside Bookings
- When the job is complete, you're charged the locked price and get a digital receipt
- Your service history updates on your vehicle's profile

## Features that make booking faster

- **Re-Book** button on the Home screen pulls your last shop and service for a one-tap booking — skips shop search and goes straight to slot picker
- **Interactive map** shows real-time slot availability for nearby shops
- **Filters** let you narrow by distance, specialty, cost, or rating
- **List view toggle** for users who prefer a list to a map

## Rescheduling and cancellations

- Reschedule by changing the time — no cancel-and-rebook needed for that
- Changing the service or the shop requires cancelling and rebooking
- Cancel from inside the booking detail — no external pages, no hoops
- Cancellation may capture a brief reason so the team can improve the experience

## If pricing changes during service

Pricing locks when you confirm. If a mechanic discovers something additional during a diagnostic, the shop has to send you a **new quote** and you have to **approve it** before any new work or charge happens. No surprise add-ons.

## How to answer common questions

**"How do I book?"**
"Pick your car, pick the service, find a mechanic on the map, pick a time, review, confirm. Six taps or fewer."

**"Can I cancel my booking?"**
"Yes, right from the app. Tap the booking and cancel. No hoops, no external pages."

**"What if the price changes after I book?"**
"Pricing locks when you confirm. If something extra comes up during the visit, the shop sends you a new quote and you have to approve it before any new work happens. No surprises."
