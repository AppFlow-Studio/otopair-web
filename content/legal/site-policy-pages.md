# Cancellations and No-Shows

otopair.com/cancellation

Plans change. Here’s exactly what happens when they do.

When you book, we place a $20 hold on your card. It’s a hold, not a charge. It’s released the moment you show up, or when you cancel in time.

|  | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Cancel more than 24 hours before | Nothing. The hold is released. |
| Cancel inside 24 hours | Up to $20 from the hold. |
| Don’t show up | Up to $20 from the hold. |
| The shop cancels | Full refund of anything paid. Hold released. |

**Why there’s a late fee.** The shop set aside a bay and a technician for you. Inside 24 hours, that time can’t be filled.

**Once work has started,** cancellation isn’t available in the app. Message the shop directly and they’ll sort it out with you. You’re responsible for authorized work done up to that point.

**Refunds** go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days. Your bank controls the last step.

**Charged in error?** Tell us in the app. We’ll look at it.

# Warranties and Service Issues

otopair.com/warranties

**Who stands behind the work.** The shop that did it. Every Partner Shop sets its own warranty terms, and we show them on the shop’s listing before you book — so you can compare shops on warranty the same way you compare them on price. Some shops offer no warranty. The listing will say so.

**What Otopair provides.** Not a warranty. We’re the platform, not the repair shop. What we do provide: a written estimate for every booking, a record of every service, and help if something goes wrong.

**If something’s wrong.** Tell the shop, and tell us in the app within 7 days of the service. We’ll help you and the shop work it out. That 7-day window is for our help — it doesn’t limit your rights against the shop, under its warranty, or under the law.

**What New York law gives you.** A shop that has given you a written estimate can’t charge more than that estimate without your consent. Every Otopair booking is a written estimate. Additional work needs your approval in the app first, and we record the moment you gave it.

# How we choose shops

otopair.com/trust

Every shop on Otopair passes the same checks before it goes live. Here’s what they are — and what they aren’t.

## What we verify

**Registered.** Every shop’s New York State DMV repair shop registration is checked against the public registry before it’s listed. You can check it yourself: [DMV repair shop lookup]({{DMV_REPAIR_SHOP_LOOKUP_URL}}).

**Insured.** Garage liability and garagekeepers coverage on file before listing, re-verified every year.

**Signed the rules.** Every shop agrees in writing: no charging above a written estimate without your approval, no conditioning service on a review, no retaliating for one.

## What that means for you

**Your car is never held over a payment.** When you pay through Otopair, the shop has agreed in writing that it cannot hold your vehicle over that payment — even if the shop and Otopair have a disagreement between us. That’s in every shop’s contract, and it’s the reason to pay through the app.

**Your price is the price.** Every booking is a written estimate. Under New York law the shop can’t charge above it without your consent, and in Otopair that consent happens in the app, on the record.

**Reviews you can trust.** Only members who completed and paid for a booking can review it. We never remove a review for being negative, and we never remove one because a shop asked.

## What verification means — and doesn’t

Partner Shops are independent businesses. Passing our checks means a shop is registered, insured, and has agreed to our rules. It isn’t a guarantee of any particular repair. You choose the shop; we make sure you have what you need to choose well — its warranty terms, its verified reviews, and its registration.
