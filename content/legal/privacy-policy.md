**1. Information We Collect**

**Information you provide to us.** We collect the following categories of information that you provide directly:

- **Account information:** your name, email address, mobile phone number, and password when you create an account.

- **Vehicle information:** the year, make, model, trim, and mileage of your vehicle(s) and, if you choose to provide them, your VIN, license plate number, and photographs of your vehicle.

- **Booking information:** the services you request, appointment dates and times, the Partner Shop you select, and any notes you attach to a booking.

- **Communications:** messages you exchange with Partner Shops through the Platform, correspondence with our support team, and reviews or ratings you submit.

- **Preferences:** your marketing and notification preferences.

- **Conversations with Oto:** the questions you ask and the information you provide to Oto, our automated assistant, together with Oto’s responses.

**Third-party sign-in.** If you create an account or sign in using Google or Apple, we receive limited profile information from that provider — your name and email address (or, if you use Apple’s Hide My Email feature, a private relay email address) — as authorized by you through that provider. We do not receive your password. Your use of a third-party sign-in service is also subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

**Payment information.** Payments on the Platform are processed by Stripe, Inc., our third-party payment processor. Your full card number is transmitted directly to Stripe and is not stored on Otopair’s systems. We receive limited payment details from Stripe, such as the transaction amount, card brand, the last four digits of your card, and payment status.

**Information collected automatically.** When you use the Platform, we collect device and usage information, including your device type and identifiers, operating system and app version, IP address, general log data, activity on the Platform (such as screens or pages viewed and searches performed), and crash and performance diagnostics. On our website, we and our service providers use cookies and similar technologies for sign-in, security, preferences, and analytics; we do not use cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising.

**Location information.** With your permission, we collect your device’s location to show you Partner Shops near you. Precise location is collected only while you are using the app and only if you grant the applicable device permission; you may instead use the Platform with approximate location or by entering a ZIP code. We do not collect location information in the background.

**Information from Partner Shops.** After a booking, the Partner Shop may provide information to the Platform about your service, such as the work performed, completion status, and invoice details, which becomes part of your service history. Partner Shops provide service records to us only for services booked through the Platform. We do not receive service records from Partner Shops for vehicles that were not serviced through an Otopair booking.

**2. How We Use Information**

We use the information described in Section 1 for the following purposes:

- **Operate the Platform:** create and maintain your account, display nearby Partner Shops, schedule bookings, process payments through Stripe, deliver receipts, and maintain your vehicle service history;

- **Provide support:** respond to your questions and help resolve disputes between users and Partner Shops;

- **Keep the Platform safe:** detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Use;

- **Improve the Platform:** understand how users interact with features, diagnose problems, and develop new features and services;

- **Operate and improve Oto:** generate Oto’s responses using the information you provide and your vehicle’s recorded history, review conversations for quality and safety, and improve the accuracy of the assistant;

- **Communicate with you:** send transactional messages such as booking confirmations, reminders, and receipts, and, unless you opt out, marketing communications about Otopair;

- **Build Data Products and Vehicle History Data:** create the Data Products and Vehicle History Data described in Section 3; and

- **Comply with law:** satisfy tax, accounting, legal, and regulatory obligations.

**3. Aggregated Data, De-Identified Data, and Vehicle History Data**

We use Platform data — for example, bookings, pricing, vehicle attributes, and service outcomes — to create aggregated and de-identified datasets and analytics products (collectively, “Data Products”), such as average repair pricing by region, service frequency by vehicle model, or parts demand trends. We may sell or license Data Products to third parties.

Data Products do not identify you. Before information is included, we remove or transform identifiers (such as your name, contact details, and precise location) so that the data cannot reasonably be linked to you, your household, or your device. We maintain technical safeguards and business processes designed to keep Data Products de-identified, we do not attempt to re-identify them, and we contractually prohibit recipients from attempting re-identification.

A documented service history is an asset for a vehicle: it supports resale value and helps future owners, buyers, and service providers understand a vehicle’s condition. We create vehicle history records (“Vehicle History Data”): records keyed to a vehicle identification number (VIN) that include vehicle attributes, mileage at the time of service, the services performed, service dates, and the identity and general location of the servicing Partner Shop. Vehicle History Data does not include your name, contact information, account identifiers, device identifiers, payment information, photographs, or the content of your communications.

We may license Vehicle History Data to vehicle-history report providers and automotive marketplaces, in each case under contracts that prohibit the recipient from using the data as a factor in establishing any consumer’s eligibility for credit, insurance, or employment, from using it in any manner that would render Otopair a consumer reporting agency, and from using it to identify, locate, or market to any vehicle owner. You may exclude your vehicle from Vehicle History Data at any time, as described in Section 6.

**4. How We Share Information**

**With the Partner Shop you book.** When you book a service, we share with that Partner Shop the information necessary to perform it: your name, contact information, vehicle information, the requested service, appointment details, and any related notes. Where you request quotes, we send your vehicle and service details — but not your name or contact information — to more than one Partner Shop in your area so that they can respond. Your name and contact information are shared only with the Partner Shop whose quote you select. Partner Shops are independent businesses; their use of your information is subject to our partner agreements and to their own privacy practices.

**With service providers.** We share information with vendors that perform services on our behalf, including payment processing (Stripe), cloud hosting, analytics, customer messaging, and the providers of the language models that power Oto. Model providers process your conversations only to generate responses and are contractually restricted from using them for their own purposes, including to train their models. Service providers are contractually restricted to using information only as necessary to provide services to Otopair. Any third party with whom we share user data — including analytics providers, third-party software development kits, service providers, and our affiliates — is contractually required to provide the same or equal protection of user data as described in this Policy.

**Data Products and Vehicle History Data.** We share, sell, or license Data Products and Vehicle History Data as described in Section 3.

**Legal, safety, and corporate transactions.** We may disclose information to comply with law or legal process; to enforce our Terms of Use; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Otopair, our users, Partner Shops, or the public; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case information may be transferred as a business asset subject to this Policy.

**Sales and sharing.** We do not sell or share Personal Information such as your name, contact details, account information, communications, precise location, or payment details, and we do not share Personal Information for cross-context behavioral advertising. As described in Section 3, we license Vehicle History Data, which is keyed to a VIN. To the extent Vehicle History Data is considered “personal information” under the law of your state, our licensing of it may constitute a “sale,” and you may opt out at any time as described in Section 6. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit under California law. Data Products consist solely of aggregated or de-identified data that does not identify you.
