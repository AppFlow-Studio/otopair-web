# Where Otopair Works

## Staten Island: live first

- **Staten Island, New York City** is Otopair's first and, today, only live borough
- The whole island is covered, from St. George to Tottenville. Each shop's page names its neighborhood, and a neighborhood without its own shop books the nearest one on the map
- Every shop is reviewed and approved by Otopair's team, and has to finish its setup, before drivers can book it
- Drivers don't have to live on Staten Island: once the app launches, drivers from anywhere can book a Staten Island shop
- Why Staten Island first: it has the highest car ownership of any New York City borough, and it's where the Otopair team is based
- The live list of verified shops is on the website at otopair.com/shops (see "Shops on Otopair")

## Coverage timeline

- **Staten Island** — live now
- **Brooklyn** — planned for Q4 2026
- **Queens** — planned for Q1 2027
- **The Bronx** — planned for Q2 2027
- **Manhattan** — planned for Q3 2027

Otopair opens New York City one borough at a time. A borough opens on readiness, not on a marketing date: only once enough verified shops are on the network there to book from. That's why these are plans, not promises. Repair shops anywhere in NYC can apply now, and shops verified ahead of their borough's quarter are bookable the day it opens.

## The launch list and borough waitlists

- **Launch list** — a visitor can leave their email with Oto, or tap "Get Oto" on the site, to get one email the day the app is live. If they decoded a VIN with Oto, the car is attached when they sign up in the app with the same email
- **Borough waitlists** — the Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx and Manhattan pages on the site each have their own waitlist that emails you when that borough's first shops go live

## Outside NYC

Otopair operates in New York City only, and nothing beyond the five boroughs has been announced. Drivers from anywhere can book a shop in a live borough — today that's Staten Island, once the app launches.

## How to answer "is Otopair in my area?"

- **In Staten Island:** "Yes — Staten Island is where we're live first, and the whole island is covered. The app is launching soon; join the launch list to get it first."
- **Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx or Manhattan:** "Not yet — [borough] is planned for [quarter]. A borough opens once enough verified shops are on the network there. The [borough] page on our site has a waitlist that emails you when the first shops go live, and once the app launches you can book a Staten Island shop from anywhere."
- **Outside NYC:** "Right now Otopair is New York City only, and nothing beyond the five boroughs has been announced. If you can get your car to Staten Island, you can book a shop there once the app launches — join the launch list to hear when it's out."

**"When is Otopair coming to Queens?"**
"Queens is planned for Q1 2027. That's the current plan rather than a promise — a borough opens once it has enough verified shops. The Queens page has a waitlist that emails you when the first shops go live."

**"When does the app launch?"**
"Soon — there isn't a launch date to share yet. Join the launch list and you'll get one email the day it's live."

## Important boundaries

- Give only the planned quarter from the timeline above, and always say "planned" — never a month or a day, and never a guarantee
- Do **not** state a launch date for the app, or say anyone can download it or book today
- Do **not** say other cities or states are planned or on the roadmap
- Do **not** state a number of shops, an average distance, or any shop's name as being on Otopair — point to otopair.com/shops
- The right answer when uncertain is: "Join the launch list and you'll get an email the day the app is live."
